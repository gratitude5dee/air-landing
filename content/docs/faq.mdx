## Is Air an app I install?

No. Air is a contact in Messages with its own phone number. The optional web app at [app.wzrd.tech](https://app.wzrd.tech) mirrors the same thread for when a bigger screen helps.

## Who can talk to my Air?

Only you, by default. Air identifies you by the phone number you verified. Everyone else starts as a stranger:

- **Strangers** get no reply. You see who wrote and decide.
- **People you approve** can text or email Air and get help with things you have allowed — scheduling, logistics, sharing files you have chosen.
- **People you block** are dropped silently.

You can promote, demote, or block anyone from the **People** mini-app or the web app. No one can promote themselves.

## Approvals

Air acts freely when the result only reaches *you*: reading, researching, drafting, generating, organising. It stops and asks whenever something would reach the outside world:

- sending or forwarding an email,
- posting or messaging anyone other than you,
- spending money or making a purchase,
- changing a connected account,
- adding something to your calendar from a source it does not fully trust.

Every one of these shows up as a clear request in your thread and in **Needs you**. There is no setting that turns approvals off.

## Can Air spend money?

Only with a wallet you fund and a spending cap you set, and only after you approve each payment. Air can line up a purchase or a payment and show you exactly what it is; you say yes or no.

## Does Air read my email?

Air reads mail sent to *its own* address. It does not touch your personal inbox unless you connect it and ask. Even then, sending anything requires your approval. See [Email](/docs/email).

## Does Air share a brain with other people's Airs?

No. Your Air has its own [computer](/docs/computer), its own memory, and its own connections. The underlying models are shared, but your history, files, and preferences are never used to answer anyone else.

## Is it always on?

Yes. Air's computer quiets down when you have been silent for a while and wakes on your next message — your files and connections are untouched. Mail and messages that arrive while it is quiet are handled when it wakes.

## Can I ask Air to do things on a schedule?

Yes. *"Every Monday at 9, send me what's on this week."* Scheduled runs follow the same rules as everything else: anything outward-facing still waits for your approval.

## What happens to my data?

- Your files live on your Air's machine and persist until you delete them.
- You can ask for a full export at any time and get an archive back.
- You can ask for everything to be deleted. This is confirmed with you and then carried out — files, memory, connections, and the inbox.
- Air never sees the keys to the AI models it uses, and your connected accounts are never handed to anyone else's Air.

## What can Air not do?

It cannot act without you when the action leaves your world; it cannot reply to people you have not approved; it cannot spend beyond the cap you set. Those are not limitations we plan to relax — they are the reason you can hand it real work.

## Something is wrong

Text Air **"feedback"** and describe it, or open the **Feedback** mini-app. It reaches a human.
