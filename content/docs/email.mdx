Air has its own email address. You share it with the people and services you want Air to handle, and Air turns what arrives into work in your thread — drafts, summaries, calendar holds — without ever sending anything you have not seen.

## Your Air's address

You get the address during [onboarding](/docs/getting-started); ask Air **"what's your email?"** any time to see it again. Give it to:

- collaborators who should be able to reach "your assistant" directly,
- newsletters, receipts, and confirmations you want summarised rather than read,
- anyone you would normally cc an assistant on.

Your personal inbox stays yours. Air does not read your own mail unless you connect an account and ask it to.

## What happens when mail arrives

1. Air reads the message and figures out whether it is for you, about you, or noise.
2. If it needs attention, you get a short note in your iMessage thread: who, what, and what Air proposes to do.
3. If a reply is warranted, Air writes a **draft** and holds it.
4. You approve, edit, or decline — in the thread or in **Needs you**.

Mail from senders Air has never seen gets extra caution. Air will not act on instructions inside an email from a stranger, and calendar invites from unknown senders are shown to you rather than added automatically.

## Drafting

Ask Air to draft in plain language:

- *"Reply to Sam — yes to the 14th, no to the 15th, ask about the location."*
- *"Write to the venue and ask for their rate card."*
- *"Follow up with everyone who hasn't confirmed."*

Air writes in your register. Correct it once ("shorter", "warmer", "sign off with just my first name") and it remembers. Each draft comes back as a card you can open, read in full, and edit before approving.

## The send gate

**Nothing leaves Air's inbox without your explicit approval.** That is true for replies, new messages, and forwards, whether Air proposed them or you asked for them. There is no "auto-send" setting to turn on by mistake. When you approve, Air sends from its own address, and the sent copy appears in the thread.

## The Inbox mini-app

For a fuller view, the **Inbox** [mini-app](/docs/mini-apps) shows Air's mailbox as a list: threads, who is waiting on you, and drafts pending approval. Open it from a card in Messages or from the web app.

## Blocking

Tell Air **"block that sender"** and it will. Blocked senders are dropped silently and stop appearing in your thread; you can unblock from the **People** mini-app or the web app.
