Some things are better as a screen than as a sentence. When that is true, Air sends a **card** in Messages that opens into a mini-app — a small, focused view for one job. Tap, act, and you are back in the thread.

## The built-in set

| Mini-app | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Onboarding** | Your first-run tour. Comes back whenever you ask. |
| **Home** | A glance at everything: what needs you, what Air is working on. |
| **Kanban** | A board for a project. Share the link and others can move cards too. |
| **To-do** | A simple list Air keeps up to date from the thread. |
| **Calendar** | Your week, with holds Air proposed and events you approved. |
| **Inbox** | Air's [email](/docs/email) mailbox: threads and drafts pending approval. |
| **People** (CRM) | Who Air knows, how much it trusts them, and recent contact. |
| **Computer** | A live view of your Air's [machine](/docs/computer). |
| **Connect** | Link and unlink the apps Air can use on your behalf. |
| **Vault** | Passwords and secrets Air may use for you, never shown in chat. |
| **Pay** and **Shop** | Approve a payment or a purchase Air has lined up. |
| **Image** and **Video** | Review and pick from generated options. |
| **Analytics**, **Ads**, **Buzz**, **Persona** | Wave features for creators: performance, campaigns, social, voice. |
| **Settings** and **Feedback** | Preferences, and a direct line to us. |

You do not have to remember any of these. Air opens the right one when it is useful, and you can always ask — *"show me the board"*, *"open the calendar"*.

## Sharing a mini-app

Some mini-apps, like Kanban and To-do, can be shared. Ask Air for a link and send it to a collaborator; they can view and edit that one board without any access to your thread, files, or anything else. Revoke it any time.

## How links work

Mini-app links are short-lived and scoped to exactly one thing. Opening a board link gets you that board and nothing more. If a link is old or has been shared onward, it simply stops working and Air can issue a fresh one.

## Kept separate on purpose

Mini-apps run on their own web address, apart from the main Air app where you sign in. That separation is deliberate: even if a mini-app had a bug, it could not reach your signed-in session, your history, or your inbox. It only ever sees the one board, list, or draft it was opened for.

## In the web app too

Every mini-app is also available at [app.wzrd.tech](https://app.wzrd.tech) with a bigger screen. Same data, same approvals; nothing you do in one place is missing from the other.
