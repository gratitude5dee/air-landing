Mini-apps are not limited to the built-in set. With **Air Create** you can make your own — described in a sentence, dropped as a file, or imported from a GitHub repository — and publish them to the store at [mini.wzrd.tech](https://mini.wzrd.tech).

## Three ways in

| You have | You do |
| --- | --- |
| An idea | Ask Air in the thread — *"make me an app that tracks my shoots"* — or describe it in the Create studio. Air builds it on your [computer](/docs/computer) and opens a preview. |
| A file or folder | Drop an `.html` file, a `.zip`, or a whole folder on the Create surface. It becomes a staged app you can preview. |
| A repository | Import from GitHub and Air packages it as a mini-app. |

Every route lands in the same place: a **studio** with your app on one side and the chat on the other. Ask for changes in plain language and the preview rebuilds.

## Preview, then publish

Nothing goes live on its own. A build stays a draft until you press **Publish** — publishing is always your decision, never the agent's.

- Each publish writes an **immutable version**. Older versions stay around, so you can roll back if a change misses.
- Your first publish makes the app **public in the store**; after that it keeps whatever visibility it has.
- You can set a **price per open** in USDC. People who open your app pay through their wallet; the charge shows up before the app loads.

## What an app can use

A mini-app is a view over your own Air. When it needs data — a board, a list, a draft — it reads and writes files on your computer, under the same approvals as everything else: an app can show you things and take input, but it cannot reach out into the world or touch another person's Air.

Apps that work in Messages keep working there after you publish them — Air can send yours as a card the same way it sends the built-in ones.

## Managing your apps

- **Installed** — apps you made or installed, in the web app under *App Store → Installed*.
- **Publish** — the create surface lists every staged draft and published version, plus any earnings on priced apps.
- **Unpublish** any time; the version history stays for your own rollback.

See [Mini-apps](/docs/mini-apps) for how cards, links, and sharing work.
