Air is in early access. Once your invitation arrives, setup takes a few minutes and happens almost entirely inside Messages.

## 1. Verify your phone number

Open the link in your invitation and enter your mobile number. You will receive a one-time code by text. This is the number Air will trust as *you* — every message from it is treated as coming from the owner, and every message from anyone else is treated as a stranger until you say otherwise.

> Use the number you actually text from. Air identifies you by your phone number, not by an account name or password.

## 2. Save Air as a contact

After verification Air texts you from its own number. Save it as a contact (most people name it "Air") so the thread stays easy to find and photos, links, and cards render properly in Messages.

## 3. Meet your Air

The first message opens a short **onboarding** card — a mini-app inside Messages that walks you through:

- what Air can do for you and how approvals work,
- a look at your Air's [computer](/docs/computer),
- an optional photo booth so Air can recognise you in reference images,
- connecting the apps you already use, and
- the address of your new [email inbox](/docs/email).

Nothing in onboarding is required beyond the phone verification. You can skip and come back — say **"connect my apps"** or **"show me onboarding again"** at any time.

## 4. Send your first text

Talk to Air the way you would talk to a sharp collaborator who has never seen your work before:

- *"Here's a moodboard for the video — pull five references in this direction."*
- *"Read this brief and tell me what's missing."*
- *"Draft a reply to the email from Sam about the shoot dates."*
- *"Build me a shot list from these notes."*

Long messages, photos, screenshots, PDFs, and links are all fine. Air keeps the thread as running context, so you can refer back to things you sent earlier.

## 5. Handle your first approval

The first time Air wants to *do* something on your behalf — send an email, post, pay — it will stop and show you exactly what it intends to do. Reply **yes**, **no**, or tell it what to change. See [FAQ: approvals](/docs/faq#approvals) for the details.

## The web app (optional)

Everything you can do in Messages you can also do at [app.wzrd.tech](https://app.wzrd.tech): the same thread, the same history, plus a **Needs you** queue for approvals and tabs for people, skills, wallet, calendar, and your Air's computer. Sign in with the same phone number. You never need to open it; it is there when a bigger screen helps.
